#### Unexpected Fall — Official Band Platform

A custom bilingual website and operational platform for Swiss alternative-rock band **Unexpected Fall**.

More than a conventional band homepage, the project combines:

- A complete German and English public website
- Music, shows, gallery, videos and lyrics
- Merchandise catalogue and Stripe Checkout
- Structured booking and contact workflows
- Protected promoter resources
- Private content and inquiry administration
- Transactional email
- Production hosting and deployment infrastructure

> **Full-stack websites built for production**  
> Custom digital products that connect public identity, application logic, private operations and deployment.

- 🎨 **Brand-led interfaces** — Every project should look like the organisation behind it, not the framework underneath it.
- ⚙️ **Full-stack application systems** — Frontend, backend, data and integrations working as one product.
- 🛒 **Commerce and booking** — Product catalogues, Stripe Checkout, inquiry flows and transactional email.
- 🔐 **Private administration** — Content, media, inquiries, maintenance controls and protected resources.

### 🕰️ The Earlier Chapter

<details>
<summary><strong>What happened to Neo RP and NeoLog?</strong></summary>

<br>

Neo RP and NeoLog were ambitious CREST development concepts from an earlier chapter.

They explored:

- Custom interface systems
- Backend application architecture
- Telemetry and data visualisation
- Gameplay and economy systems
- Product identity
- Long-term platform design

Neither project ultimately became a complete public product.

They are therefore no longer presented as active releases or used to define the current direction of CREST.

Their value was developmental. The interface discipline, systems thinking and technical experience developed through those projects now feed directly into production web development.

> **The prototypes stayed behind. The experience did not.**

</details>
